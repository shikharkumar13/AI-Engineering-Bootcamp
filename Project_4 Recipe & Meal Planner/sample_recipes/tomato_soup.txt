Tomato Soup

Serves: 4
Total time: 30 minutes
Cuisine: American

Ingredients:
- 2 cans crushed tomatoes (14 oz each)
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- crusty bread, for serving

Instructions:
Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook
for 5 minutes until softened, then add the garlic and cook for 1 more
minute. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, vegetable broth, and salt, and bring
to a simmer. Cook uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Use an
immersion blender to puree the soup until smooth, then stir in the heavy
cream. Simmer for 2 more minutes and serve hot with crusty bread.
