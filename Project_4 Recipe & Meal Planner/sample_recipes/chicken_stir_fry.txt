Chicken Stir Fry

Serves: 4
Total time: 25 minutes
Cuisine: Chinese-American

Ingredients:
- 1 lb chicken breast, diced
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 carrot, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups cooked rice, for serving

Instructions:
Heat the oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat. Add the diced chicken
and cook until browned, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and onion and stir
for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the broccoli and carrot, then stir fry for
5 more minutes until the vegetables are crisp-tender. Pour in the soy sauce
and salt, toss everything together, and cook for 2 more minutes. Serve hot
over the cooked rice.
