Veggie Tacos

Serves: 3
Total time: 20 minutes
Cuisine: Mexican

Ingredients:
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 8 small corn tortillas
- 1 avocado, sliced
- lime wedges, for serving

Instructions:
Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and bell
pepper and cook for 5 minutes until softened. Add the garlic and cumin and
cook for 1 more minute until fragrant. Stir in the black beans and salt, and
cook for 3 minutes until heated through, mashing some of the beans lightly
with the back of a spoon. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or microwave, then
fill each with the bean and pepper mixture. Top with avocado slices and
serve with lime wedges.
