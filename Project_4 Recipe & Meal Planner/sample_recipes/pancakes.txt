Pancakes

Serves: 4
Total time: 20 minutes
Cuisine: American

Ingredients:
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1.25 cups milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp melted butter
- maple syrup, for serving

Instructions:
In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
In a separate bowl, whisk the milk, egg, and melted butter together, then
pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir just until
combined (a few lumps are fine). Heat a lightly greased griddle or nonstick
pan over medium heat. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake and cook
until bubbles form on the surface, about 2 minutes, then flip and cook for
another 1-2 minutes until golden. Serve warm with maple syrup.
