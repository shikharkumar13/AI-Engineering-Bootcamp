Spaghetti Bolognese

Serves: 4
Total time: 45 minutes
Cuisine: Italian

Ingredients:
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cans crushed tomatoes (14 oz each)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 12 oz spaghetti
- grated parmesan, for serving

Instructions:
Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic
and cook until softened, about 4 minutes. Add the ground beef and cook,
breaking it up with a spoon, until browned. Stir in the crushed tomatoes,
salt, and oregano, then simmer uncovered for 25 minutes, stirring
occasionally. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water
according to the package directions and drain. Toss the pasta with the
sauce and serve topped with grated parmesan.
